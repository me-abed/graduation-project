Frequently Asked

Questions

Find answers to common questions about Visionners including its features, usage, pricing, safety, and how to get support.

What is Visionners?

VISIONNEERS is an AI-powered system that converts 2D images of statues and dental structures into detailed, interactive 3D digital models for visualization and analysis.

What can Visionners do?

The system analyzes a 2D image, estimates depth and structural features, and reconstructs a realistic 3D model with accurate geometry and surface details — ready for web interaction.

What are the key advantages of Visionners?

VISIONNEERS offers fast and accurate 3D reconstruction from a single 2D image, reducing the need for manual modeling. It combines AI-based depth estimation with realistic texture generation to produce detailed 3D models. The system supports both artistic (statues) and dental applications, provides interactive web-based visualization, and significantly saves time and effort compared to traditional 3D design workflows.

How to use Visionners?

On Visionners , all it takes to generate a 3D model is uploading a single image. Supported formats include .JPG, .JPEG, .PNG, and .WebP, with a maximum file size of 20MB.

What 3D file formats does Visionners support?

Visionners supports downloading models in multiple formats, including .glb, .mlb, which are compatible with mainstream 3D modeling software (such as Blender, Unity and Maya) and 3D printing software.

Is Visionners safe and legal?

Visionners adopts industry-standard security measures to protect user data, ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all uploaded content. For details, please review our Privacy Policy.

Are the 3D models generated on Visionners private?

Yes. By default, all models you generate are private and visible only to you. You can generate a share link on the model details page to share them publicly.

How to contact us for support?

Join our official Discord community to interact directly with other users and our team, getting the latest news and support. Email us at support@Visionners.ai for answers and help.

Why do subscription credits reset at the end of the billing period?

Subscription credits reset at the end of each billing period to ensure fair usage and predictable access for all users. This system encourages you to use your credits within the paid period, prevents unused credits from accumulating indefinitely, and helps maintain consistent service levels and resource availability. If your plan allows roll-overs or upgrades, those details will be shown in your subscription terms.
